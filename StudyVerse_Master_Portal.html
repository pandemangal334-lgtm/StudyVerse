02 / The method

Study like a system, not a mood

A small workflow to turn reading into usable knowledge.

01 · UnderstandAsk “why does this work?” before memorising a formula.
02 · RetrieveClose the notes and explain the idea from memory.
03 · ApplySolve one question without looking, then check the steps.
03 / Focus room

One honest 25-minute session

Choose one concept. Put distractions away. Work until the timer ends, then take a short break.
