Formula box

  • Page fault: requested page memory mein nahi hai.
  • Page hit: requested page already frame mein hai.
  • Total head movement: har consecutive head position ka absolute difference add karo.
  • Average head movement: total movement ÷ number of requests.
  • Seek time: total head movement × seek time per track (if given).

SSTF, PDF page 46 Example 2: From head 30 with requests 45,23,89,12,67, the stated sum 7+11+33+22+22 is 95, not 105.
SSTF, PDF page 46 Example 3: Requests 150,30,90,10,70 from head 50 produce 180 tracks by SSTF (ties may depend on tie-breaking policy), therefore 18 ms at 0.1 ms/track—not 260 tracks / 26 ms.
